Forex, Gold & Crypto Profit/Loss Calculator

Use this free trading calculator to work out profit and loss on forex pairs, gold (XAUUSD), silver (XAGUSD), and crypto trades before or after you place them. Covers major pairs like EUR/USD, GBP/USD, and USD/JPY, cross pairs like EUR/GBP and GBP/JPY, exotic pairs including USD/PKR and USD/INR, and 15+ cryptocurrencies. Enter your entry price, exit price, and lot size to see your exact P/L in US dollars — search any instrument by typing its name or symbol.

Position Size & Risk Calculator

Proper position sizing is the core of forex risk management. This calculator works out the maximum lot size to trade based on your account balance, risk percentage, and stop-loss distance — so a stop-loss hit only costs the percentage of your account you choose, across forex, metals, and crypto.

How to calculate forex profit and lot size

Forex profit is calculated as the price movement multiplied by your position size in units, then converted to your account currency. For USD-quoted pairs like EUR/USD or GBP/USD, this is straightforward. For USD-base pairs like USD/JPY or USD/PKR, and cross pairs like EUR/GBP that don't involve USD directly, an extra currency conversion step is needed — this calculator handles that conversion automatically using live exchange rates.

Calculations are estimates using standard contract sizes and live exchange rates for currency conversion — always confirm exact contract specs and margin rules with your own broker. Not financial advice.
