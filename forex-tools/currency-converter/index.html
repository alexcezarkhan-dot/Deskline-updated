What is DeskTerminal's Currency Converter?

DeskTerminal's Currency Converter converts between world currencies using live, real-time exchange rates — the same rate data behind the rest of DeskTerminal's market tools. Traders use it to check a rate before funding an account or comparing a broker's quote; everyone else uses it for ordinary, everyday conversions.

Of roughly 180 currencies recognized as legal tender worldwide, 137 are shown here with a full name and country flag, searchable either way. Every currency our live rate provider offers can still be converted, flag or no flag — the display simply focuses on making the most common ones instantly recognizable.

There's no sign-up, no account required, and no catch — it's part of a free trading platform, and the converter is one small piece of that.

How to use this currency converter

Enter an amount, choose your "from" currency, choose your "to" currency, and the result updates instantly. The swap button reverses the pair in one click, and each Popular Conversion below jumps straight to that exact pair.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this currency converter free?

Yes — no sign-up, and rates refresh automatically every 5 minutes.

How many currencies does it support?

Whatever the live rate provider offers, generally upward of 150 — far beyond the handful of majors some simpler converters limit you to.

How often are exchange rates updated?

Every 5 minutes, automatically, from a live rate feed.

What's the difference between the mid-market rate and what my broker actually offers?

This shows the real mid-market rate — the true midpoint between global buy and sell prices, the same benchmark you'd see quoted in financial news. Brokers and banks add a spread on top as part of how they earn revenue, so your actual exchange will land slightly less favorably than the number shown here. Treat this as a planning reference, not a guaranteed execution price.

Can I use this for trading, not just travel money?

Yes. Checking a live forex rate before placing a trade uses the exact same feed as converting money for a trip — there's no separate "trading mode."

Does it cover less common currencies, or only USD, EUR, and GBP?

Search any supported currency by name or code — the coverage extends well past the major pairs most people default to.

Why does the rate here differ slightly from Google or my bank?

Every converter pulls from its own live data provider, and rates shift by the second in real markets — small gaps between sources are normal, usually fractions of a percent, rarely enough to matter for everyday decisions.

Can I convert a large amount, like for an international transfer?

Yes, any amount — the mid-market rate applies proportionally regardless of size. A real transfer service will still apply its own rate and fees on top of this reference figure.

What is the interbank rate, and should I use it?

It's the rate banks use trading currency amongst themselves, with no markup — the cleanest available reference for what a currency is genuinely worth right now, which is what this tool shows. Useful for judging how fair a broker's quote is; just don't expect a retail counter to match it exactly, since their margin sits on top of it.

Can I search by currency name, not just the code?

Yes — "Euro" and "EUR" both find the same result.
