Frequently Asked Questions

What is DeskTerminal?

A free, real-time markets platform covering forex, gold and silver, crypto, and major stock indices — combining live prices, an economic calendar, breaking financial news, and a set of practical trading tools in one place, without requiring an account to use most of it.

What are the trading sessions shown on the homepage?

The four major forex trading sessions — Sydney, Tokyo, London, and New York — shown against the current time, so you can see which markets are genuinely open right now and where session overlaps create the highest typical liquidity.

What is the Market Scanner?

A real currency strength ranking, computed from price snapshots we collect ourselves across the major pairs — showing at a glance which currencies are genuinely strengthening or weakening against the basket right now, not a static or estimated figure.

Do I need to create an account to use DeskTerminal?

No — live prices, the calendar, news, and every calculator tool work without signing in. An account is only needed for optional features like saving a personal watchlist or submitting a news story.

Is DeskTerminal free to use?

Yes, completely free — no subscription, no paywall on the core tools and data.

How is DeskTerminal different from Forex Factory or Investing.com?

DeskTerminal is newer and smaller, but built with AI woven directly into the experience — automatic news impact classification, an AI assistant explaining market moves, and AI-generated analysis on every instrument page — alongside the same core tools traders expect from an established platform.

What is DeskAi?

DeskTerminal's built-in AI assistant, available across the site to explain price moves, market context, and specific tools in plain language — genuinely useful as a starting point for understanding what's happening, though never a substitute for your own research or financial advice.
