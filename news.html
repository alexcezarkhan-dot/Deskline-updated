Frequently Asked Questions

Where does DeskTerminal's forex news come from?

Headlines are pulled live from trusted, established financial news publishers — including InvestingLive (formerly ForexLive), MarketWatch, Yahoo Finance, Kitco News, CoinDesk, Marketaux, and Finnhub. Every headline links to the original article.

What are Hot Stories?

Hot Stories is a curated selection of the most important headlines, capped at 15. It automatically shows the latest breaking story until an editor manually highlights a specific story as more important, similar to how major financial news aggregators curate their top section.

How often is the news updated?

The news feed refreshes automatically each time you load the page, pulling the latest available headlines from the source publishers.

Is DeskTerminal's news free to read?

Yes, all news headlines are free to browse with no account or sign-up required.

Can I filter news by source?

Yes, use the filter tabs above the headline list to view news from all sources or a specific publisher only.
