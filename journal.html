Trading journal FAQ

What is a trading journal?

A log of your trades (entry, exit, stop loss, size, result and notes) that you review to find what works and fix repeated mistakes.

Is the DeskTerminal trading journal free?

Yes. Log trades free in your browser, or sign in to sync them to your DeskTerminal account across devices.

Which instruments can I journal?

60+ instruments: forex pairs, gold and silver, crypto, oil and major indices like US30 and NAS100, plus any custom symbol.

What performance metrics does it calculate?

Net P&L, win rate, profit factor, expectancy in R, average win and loss, max drawdown, win and loss streaks, plus an equity curve and P&L calendar.

Are my trades private, and can I export them?

Signed in, your trades are protected so only you can see them. You can also export a JSON backup or download any trade as a PDF report.
