Frequently Asked Questions

Is this real-time market data?

Yes — forex, gold, silver, and crypto prices update live from real market data sources, and charts are powered by TradingView. There's no manual refresh needed; prices update automatically as the market moves.

Is DeskTerminal free to use?

Yes, DeskTerminal is completely free to use, including live prices, charts, the trade calculator, economic calendar, and AI-powered market analysis — no account required to browse.

What markets does DeskTerminal cover?

DeskTerminal covers forex (major, minor, and exotic currency pairs), precious metals (gold and silver), major cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin and Ethereum, and global stock indices including the S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100.

Can I save a watchlist on DeskTerminal?

Yes — sign in with Google or Facebook, then click the star icon on any instrument to add it to your personal watchlist, which is saved privately to your account.
